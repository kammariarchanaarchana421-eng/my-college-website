About Our College

Pragnya Group of Degree & P.G. Colleges is a private educational institution located in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, affiliated with Osmania University. It offers both Co-Ed and Women's degree programs.

History Of Pragnya Degree College

Pragnya Group of Degree & P.G. Colleges (Chandanagar, Hyderabad) was established in the year 2010. The institution was founded with a central mission: to make high-quality, job-oriented higher education affordable and accessible to regular students
The college was established under the leadership and vision of its Chairman, S. Sreenivasa Reddy (M.Com, LLB). Backed by a management team with extensive experience in the educational sector, the institution set out to bridge the gap between traditional academic degrees and modern industry demands.
