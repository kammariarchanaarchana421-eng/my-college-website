Pragnya Degree College Details

Pragnya Degree & P.G. College, located in Chandanagar, Hyderabad, was established in 2010 with the aim of providing quality education to students. The college provides various facilities, including spacious classrooms, computer laboratories, a library, science laboratories, CCTV surveillance and placement training. It offers undergraduate courses such as BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.Sc Computer Science and B.Sc Data Science, along with postgraduate courses such as M.Com and M.Sc Computer Science. The college is affiliated with Osmania University, Hyderabad, and follows the university's academic curriculum and examination system. It is listed as a private unaided affiliated college with AISHE code C-25538
